ABOUT THE TEMPLE

A Sacred Centre of Devotion

The Veda Maatha Gayatri Devi Temple stands in Rega Village, Lakkavarapukota Mandal, Vizianagaram District, Andhra Pradesh. Dedicated to Goddess Gayatri, the temple is particularly distinguished by the worship of her Panchamukha form. The peaceful surroundings create a setting for devotion, silence and spiritual contemplation.

THE DIVINE PRESENCE

Veda Mata — The Mother of the Vedas

Goddess Gayatri is traditionally worshipped as Veda Mata, the Mother of the Vedas, and as the divine embodiment of the sacred Gayatri Mantra.

THE SACRED GAYATRI MANTRA

The Prayer of Divine Illumination

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं
भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥

Om Bhur Bhuvah Svah · Tat Savitur Varenyam · Bhargo Devasya Dhimahi · Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat

“We meditate upon the divine radiance of Savitur, the source of all creation. May that supreme light illuminate our intellect and guide us on the path of righteousness.”

PANCHAMUKHA GAYATRI DEVI

The Five-Faced Divine Form

The Panchamukha form of Gayatri Devi is traditionally understood through five sacred faces, each carrying profound symbolic associations.

01

Eastern Face

The Light of Awakening

New beginnings · Awakening · Knowledge · Illumination

Traditionally associated with Brahma, the principle of creation.
02

Southern Face

The Power of Protection

Strength · Courage · Protection · Divine power

Traditionally associated with Narasimha, the divine protector.
03

Western Face

The Wisdom of Transformation

Transformation · Change · Preservation · Cycles of existence

Traditionally associated with Vishnu, the preserver.
04

Northern Face

The Journey Within

Inner wisdom · Reflection · Spiritual awareness · Deeper understanding

Traditionally associated with Shiva, the principle of transformation.
05

Upward Face

The Path to Transcendence

Spiritual elevation · Transcendence · Divine consciousness · Ultimate reality

Traditionally associated with Ishvara, the Supreme Divine.

HOW TO REACH

Your Journey to the Divine Begins Here

The Veda Maatha Gayatri Devi Temple is conveniently accessible from Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram, Kothavalasa and Musiram. Whether your journey begins in a bustling city or a quiet village, the final stretch towards Rega leads into a peaceful rural landscape and towards the sacred presence of Gayatri Devi.

VisakhapatnamVisakhapatnam → Kothavalasa → Devarapalli Road → Musiram → Rega Village → Temple
VizianagaramVizianagaram → Kothavalasa → Devarapalli Road → Musiram → Rega Village → Temple
KothavalasaKothavalasa → Devarapalli Road → Musiram → Rega Village → Temple
MusiramMusiram → Rega Village → Temple

By Train

Vizianagaram Railway Station — Approx. 25 km. From Vizianagaram, travel towards Kothavalasa, continue along Devarapalli Road, then proceed towards Musiram and Rega Village.

By Road

The temple can be approached from Visakhapatnam and Vizianagaram through Kothavalasa and Devarapalli Road, followed by Musiram and Rega Village.
